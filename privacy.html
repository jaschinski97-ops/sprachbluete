Privacy Policy

for the app “Sprachblüte” · Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short

Sprachblüte does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. Your learning progress stays on your device. The app only connects to GitHub when you download a language pack.

Controller

Data on your device

Learning progress, settings (such as app language and day/night mode) and downloaded language packs are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us or to third parties and are deleted when you delete the app. A backup file is only created if you export it yourself.

Microphone

The microphone is used only in the speaking exercises, after you tap “Start recording” and grant access. The recording stays in your device’s memory, is analysed there (pitch and duration) and then discarded. No audio is stored or sent anywhere.

Downloading language packs (GitHub)

When you tap “Download”, the app fetches the list of available packs and the pack file from GitHub (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). As with any web request, GitHub receives technically necessary connection data, in particular your IP address, the time and the requested file, and processes it under its own privacy statement. We receive no data about you from GitHub. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the content you requested). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

App Store

When you download or update the app from the App Store, Apple processes data under the Apple Privacy Policy, which is outside our control.

Speech output

For texts without their own audio clip the app uses your device’s built-in speech output. This runs in the operating system; the app sends no data to us.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. As we store no data about you, we can usually only confirm that none exists. Contact us at the email address above.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that involves other data, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
