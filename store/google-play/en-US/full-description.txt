Sprachblüte helps you learn Thai, English, Spanish or German – free, without an account and without ads.

CHAPTERS BUILT ON REAL DIALOGUES
Every chapter starts with an everyday conversation: at a restaurant, shopping, getting around. You listen to and read the dialogue, learn its words, practise the rule behind it and finally write and speak yourself.

WORDS THAT STICK
Around 2,000 words per language in three levels: beginner, intermediate and advanced. Flashcards bring each word back at the right moment, before you forget it. Words come with pictures and audio clips.

SPEAKING WITH FEEDBACK
In shadowing you repeat a sentence and see your sentence melody right next to the model's. The app points out the most noticeable spot with one concrete tip, and you can listen to the model and your own recording in turn. Word recognition runs on your device; your recordings never leave the phone.

THAI WITH TONES
Thai words are shown in Thai script with romanisation. Foundation chapters introduce the script, the tones and numbers.

MANY WAYS TO PRACTISE
Listening, reading & writing, speaking, grammar and vocabulary: dictation, sentence building, reading texts, role play, word lists and a daily round.

OFFLINE AND PRIVATE
Download audio and pictures once per language as a language pack; after that everything works without internet. Your progress stays on your device. The app collects no data.

The interface is available in ten languages: German, English, Spanish, Thai, Arabic, Chinese, Hindi, French, Bengali and Portuguese.
